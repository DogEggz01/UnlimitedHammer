**Main Feature**
- Skip "on the floor limit" and "cannot be moving" check for nail down check.
  - This means the nailed item will stay in the air, even if you move the things underneath away.
  - Item nailed in the air will follow ship and still have weight
- Show nail progress as percentage on item
  - Hammer timer actually follow the hammer itself, so you can progress on other item first and insta nail the item you want.
- Make model ship and flower pots nailable
- Can toggle the mod off in Configurator

This mod made you nail the things at whatever position you want.

- You could store nail timer to 99%, hold something to your desire position, drop it and instant nail it.
- Stack crates to your desired height and place the item you want to nail on top of the crate. Nail the item and remove crate.
- Use small table/chair as wall blocker (could nail it to the place first), place painting/map on the surface of table/chair, used stored nail timer to nail the painting/map before it drop. This can avoid painting/map texture not shown correctly on wall. Althought you will likely have small space between wall and painting/map

Made for interior design enthusiastic or simply to make life easier
